# Zoomable paper: an interactive reading demo

A demonstration of how a journal article could be read at different lengths and depths.
Open it on an iPad in Safari and pinch or swipe the text.

- Pinch out or in: longer or shorter, for the whole paper.
- Swipe a paragraph left or right: more technical or plainer, for that paragraph.
- On a computer: trackpad pinch and two-finger swipe, or the + − ← → keys. Press 0 to return to the published text.

The article is Reid M, Abdool-Karim Q, Geng E, Goosby E (2021) *How will COVID-19 transform global health post-pandemic? Defining research and investment opportunities and priorities*. PLoS Med 18(3): e1003564. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1003564. It is reproduced under the Creative Commons Attribution License (CC BY 4.0).

The published text is the centre position of the reader. The other versions were generated by an AI model from the published text. References 14 onwards were found with Consensus and checked against PubMed in November 2026; they post-date the article and are not part of it.

This is not a PLOS product. Inspired by an interaction concept shared by Fabian (@fabrohl).
